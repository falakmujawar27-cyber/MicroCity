Proposed workflow

Two loops, one evidence trail: plan the campus, resolve the report

The planning loop runs on records and analysis; the issue loop runs on reports and work notes. Both write to the same audit trail.
Loop A

Planning and decision loop

Estates prepares · Administrator decides
1

Record or import

Buildings, rooms and assets are entered on screen or imported from CSV and Excel. Every record states its source and whether the figure is recorded or estimated. Nothing enters the planning data without provenance.

Source + provenance
2

Validate and store

The server checks field types, dates and that each row belongs to the signed-in college. Imports are mapped per sheet, previewed, then appended or replaced behind an explicit safeguard. Rejected rows are reported, never silently dropped.

Import history
3

Compute

Deterministic rules compare demand with recorded room capacity, total electricity and water use from meter readings, and maintenance priority from category, severity and age. Every figure is arithmetic over stored records, not a model guess.

Rule-based figures
4

Frame the gap

Each gap is written in plain language: what is short, by how much, and which recorded options could close it. An option keeps its own note on cost or effort only where that note was entered.

Options + trade-offs
5

Decide

Only the administrator approves, rejects or defers. The decision keeps its actor, its time and a required note, so a reader months later can see why the choice was made.

Decision log
6

Plan, report, print

The plan and its print view carry the basis of every figure: recorded, estimated, illustrative or project assumption. An export is a point-in-time snapshot and says so.

Point-in-time export
Loop B

Issue and maintenance loop

Any member reports · Estates assigns · Technician closes
1

Report a problem

Any signed-in member files a report in three steps: what is broken, where it is (a room, or “not sure”), and a short description with an optional photo. The form returns a ticket number at once.

Ticket number
2

Triage and assign

Estates or the administrator sets severity and category, sees similar earlier reports, and assigns a technician. Assignment and priority are recorded choices, never inferred by a model.

Owner + priority
3

Accept the job

The technician sees only assigned work, ordered under To do, In progress and Done. Another technician’s queue is not visible to them.

Work queue
4

Work in progress

Each status change is timestamped and appears on a timeline the reporter can also read. A ticket cannot change state without an actor and a time.

Live status
5

Complete with evidence

Moving a job to Done requires a work note and accepts an optional photo. The record stays open to audit and to later inspection.

Work note
6

Reporter tracks it

The reporter follows the same ticket under My reports and sees every update on it. Nothing is closed silently and nothing disappears from the queue.

Shared record
Audit + integrity

Every create, update, import, role change and decision appends an audit entry with actor and time.

Missing is never zero.
No AI can approve or assign.
